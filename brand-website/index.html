The Challenge

Problem Statement

Temperature-sensitive medications — insulin, vaccines, biologics — require strict cold-chain management throughout their entire lifecycle. A single temperature excursion can silently degrade a drug's efficacy, putting patients at serious risk without any visible warning sign.

According to the WHO, nearly 50% of vaccines are wasted globally each year due to temperature failures. Insulin, which must be stored between 2°C and 8°C, becomes ineffective when exposed to heat — yet most patients have no way to verify their storage conditions at home.

There is a clear need for an affordable, connected monitoring device that gives patients and caregivers real-time visibility into medication storage conditions, before damage occurs.

~50% of vaccines wasted globally due to temperature failures (WHO)
2–8°C insulin storage range — excursions render it ineffective
Zero affordable home solutions for real-time medication monitoring

Our Approach

Proposed Solution

ThermoGo is an IoT ecosystem built around three principles — Simplicity, Configurability, and Accessibility. It consists of three integrated components: the ThermoGo Device, the ThermoGo Mobile App, and the ThermoGo Cloud.

The ESP32-WROOM-32 device continuously samples temperature and humidity via the BME280 sensor, buffering up to 10 readings locally before syncing to Supabase over Wi-Fi. An adaptive sleep algorithm keeps deep-sleep current below 100 µA, targeting a battery life exceeding 180 days.

Hardware Design

The Circuit Board

The ThermoGo PCB is a custom 2-layer board designed in Altium Designer, optimised for compact form factor, signal integrity, and minimal power draw. All critical components — ESP32-WROOM-32, BME280, ADXL345, TP4056 charger, and MT3608 boost converter — are placed on the top layer with clear domain separation.

The board features a dedicated ground plane, decoupling capacitors on every power rail, and careful RF keepout zone routing around the ESP32 antenna. Total BOM cost for the prototype is 70.80 TND.
